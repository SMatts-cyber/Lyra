Lyra Privacy Policy

Lyra is a private assistant used only by its developer, Sunley Mathew. It reads the developer's own Gmail and Calendar, only to read messages aloud and remind about meetings.

No data is collected from other people, shared with third parties, or sold. Data stays on the developer's own devices, and only the minimum text needed for a request is sent to an AI service.
